Privacy policy.

How we collect, use and protect your personal and health information.

This is placeholder text for a concept project. A real clinic must have its privacy policy written or reviewed against the Privacy Act 1988 and the Australian Privacy Principles before launch.

What we collect

  • Contact details you give us: name, phone, email and preferred appointment times.
  • Health information collected during your appointments, stored in our practice management system.
  • Health fund, Medicare or DVA details needed to process claims.

How we use it

To book and manage your appointments, provide treatment, process claims and contact you about your care. We don't sell your information or use it for unrelated marketing.

Website enquiries

The call-back form asks only for your name, phone, email and preferred time. Please don't include health details in it; we'll discuss those with you directly.

Storage and access

Records are stored securely with access limited to clinic staff. You can ask to see or correct your information at any time by calling (07) 5550 1234 or emailing hello@fernmoorphysio.com.au.

Complaints

If you have a concern about how we've handled your information, contact us first. If it isn't resolved, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC).
